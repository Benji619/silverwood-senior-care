What kind of home is Silverwood?＋

1st Silverwood Senior Care Home LLC is a licensed adult family home in Lynnwood, Washington, with capacity for five residents. It offers personal care and 24-hour supervision in a residential setting.

Can our family come for a tour?＋

Absolutely. Call (425) 967-5967 or (206) 334-8177, or email taduadiss@gmail.com to arrange a visit. We’ll agree on a time so you can see the spaces and ask questions.

What support is available?＋

Services include daily personal care, medication management, meals, housekeeping, laundry, activities, and supervision. Contact us to discuss memory care needs, nursing support, and whether our home is suitable for your loved one.

What payment options can we discuss?＋

Ask us about private pay, Medicaid, and long-term care insurance benefits. Please contact us to confirm current acceptance, coverage arrangements, costs, and availability.

Are there rooms available right now?＋

Availability changes. Call or email us for current openings, room options, and next steps. We’ll discuss your family’s needs and arrange a visit if appropriate.

Where are you located?＋

Our home is at 2406 207th Pl SW, Lynnwood, WA 98036, in Snohomish County. Use the directions link below to plan your visit.
